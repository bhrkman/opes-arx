THE KIER BASTILLE: HOUSES COMPETE ON THE WAY OUT

The blind sheet made the Bastille a gamble; nothing made it a market. A claim was free and
first refusal went to whoever was shortest of people, so a good gamble cost what a bad one
did. And the price was the sheet: the card carried the man's open-market wage, which reads
his stat total at r = 0.92. The numbers were redacted and the wage said them.

- The auction was built as the first answer and STRUCK A SECOND TIME. The prep calendar's
  own comment records the T4 auction being moved by designer ruling; it was rebuilt past
  that line. A lot of people bid for with credits is chattel whatever the paper says. The
  ruling now lives in recruitment.json where it cannot be read past.
- What houses compete on is HIS road out. Each offers a term — the sentence as written, or
  release after fewer Divides — and every Divide forgiven is bought from the Kier
  (remission_per_divide: 3,500, a starting point). He takes the shortest road; between
  equal roads, the house whose people come home (weighOffer, money held constant).
- The Kier's scale, ruled in data: a flat wage (kier_wage_monthly 120) and a flat
  processing fee (600). Nothing on the sheet moves any price but the sentence; the
  potential_premium that read the scout estimate into young lots is retired.
- The manager's house is in the room like the others but never buys remission unasked.
  The card: Honour the Sentence · Release After N · ₡. The recap names the road taken.
- sim/audit_bastille.cjs gates it: the Divides AI houses buy must not track the hidden
  stats (r = 0.04, bounded on the count of men); wage, fee and remission charged exactly.
  Its first cut lied — a fresh fleet per pass is season one per pass, the same six men
  forty times. One fleet, seasons advancing.
- measure_value.cjs crashed on the blind record since the redaction, and multiplied a
  yearly ask by twelve: the ₡27,612 Natural-Born year in PROJECT.md is ₡2,301. Ratios held.
- claimPrisoner/claimsOf/_bastilleClaims and BASTILLE_WAGE_FRAC are gone; contract.sentence
  is kept beside divides_required so the sheet can show both.

--- and the first money pass, same session ---

THE BOOKS, READ THROUGH THE LOCK. Pay was charged about twice (the monthly wage line beside
the S15 retainer and purse) and the entry fee twice (season loop beside settlement). Ruled:
the monthly line is the retainer spread over the year, the purse lands at the muster, the
lock posts the Divide month's twelfth; the entry fee is the season loop's alone. Measured
whole, the fleet cleared +₡178k a house a year before it fought — two incomes, grant and
gate, each sized to the same bill.

Four levers, none all the way: founding_mix 70/10/20 ruled in recruitment.json (was 45/35/20
in roster.js — a third of every founding roster mercenaries); salary_base 400→600 and the Kier
wage 120→180 with it; board grants halved; the gate cut a quarter. Fleet average +₡12k before
the Divide; hard houses negative; the Divide's money decides the year. Kit (₡8k a year, the
allowance caps value not spend) is the next lever. measure_economy.cjs reads every house
through the lock. Combat snapshots blessed; measure_fight holds. drive.cjs's training check
read a cohort mean the drive's own trades moved; it reads each hand against itself now.

--- and the founded house as the yardstick, same session ---

The only house a manager can play is the one he founds. It opened as the fleet's average in
every number, reputation included, and took ₡93k a year at the door before doing anything;
its grant was the largest in the fleet. founderProfile now lives in the engine (the desk's
blankSlate calls it): liked at home, unknown to the fleet. LEAN_GRANT 150k → 90k: a careful
founder runs −₡5k, −₡1k, +₡8k over three years before any fighting, the crowd doing the
growing. The eight are funded relative to him — grantFor = LEAN_GRANT × (2 − 0.25 ×
difficulty) — and the hand-written funding_base figures are gone from the profiles.
measure_economy.cjs founds a house as the desk does, plays it carefully, and reports it first.

--- and the founder played through the fights, same session ---

measure_founder.cjs follows a carefully played founded house through six careers of three
years, the whole year. Three more double charges: the dead paid for twice (an estimate at the
lock beside the per-contract pension — the estimate is gone); a trade's credits moved twice
on the page (the poster moves them now); and the fleet's own trades were selling the manager's
fighters, unasked and off the books (the fleet deals among the other seven, on the books).
Measured: −₡22k, −₡17k, then the Divide decides, ±₡150k. What decides it is deaths — 37% of
everyone dropped, every year — and ransoms, not wages. Kit is the same for everybody.

--- and the table, same session ---

THE PRINCIPAL IS A PARTY TO THE DEAL. A joiner computed both sides' limits — the principal's
private ceiling included — and the deal was struck; the manager's banner was bought for him.
Now the joiner asks from an estimate (±15%, once per pair) and can be refused; an AI principal
answers (considerTake); a manager's banner waits on him — the ask stands on the window with
their terms, what refusing costs each side, and three answers. Two small terms inside the
valuation, by ruling small: spite (a fraction of what finishing them would cost THEM, by
culture and grudge) and goodwill (a traditional house pays a little to take a beaten rival
in). Three memories — spared, generous_terms, left_to_die — raised on the principal and
remembered by the other house's people, and the living regard between houses now moves the
price (±20%) where only the profiles' written relationships did before.

--- and the table's second pass (Sep 15) ---

Whom to approach: every banner ranked by its worth to THIS house, warmed by regard, discounted
for a grudge, lifted for kingmaking (rankBanners) — not the two strongest, always. When: a
patient house with a live chance sits a window out (actsThisWindow). What to ask for: terms
composed from what each side values — a site or category the house wants more than the banner
first, then credits, then share (composeTerms); the coin toss for a cut in kind is gone.
Principals reach out: each AI banner courts the house that would lift its take most, at a
little over its guessed floor (considerInvite); 20 invitations in eight Divides, 10 taken.
The strike is one function. Measured on the way: the spoiler is a body or so against gains
of ₡150k, and is not what moves anyone.

--- and the table's third pass: the house you are engaged with ---

RULED: the table is for the house hunting you or the one you are beating, not the
leaderboard; and teaming up for alliance's sake is what the Aleas and the fans punish.
Contact is recorded per pair (noteContact: fights, who lost to whom, who hunts whom) and
read by the table summed over a banner. Whom to approach and whom to court is led by
contact; a banner never met is worth half; regard is out of the ranking. A cold alliance
costs both sides at the table and is remembered (cold_alliance). Measured: 23 of 24 deals
between houses whose squads had met. The manager's strip says who is on him.

--- and the regret instrument ---

measure_regret.cjs: every AI decision at the table passes through `decide` (act or hold,
which banner, accept or refuse); the instrument replays a Divide from its seed with one
decision forced the other way and scores the house's outcome, signed. Its first cut could not
go negative and read principals as overpaying; corrected. Finding: the principal's
accept/refuse is the only decision that binds and the AI takes it ₡121k better than the
alternative on average (8 of 14 right); the joiner's decisions are moot 20 of 20, because only
~8% of house-pairs can ever close — a question about the table's shape, not the AI.

--- and the word "house" removed ---

They are OAs — corporations — and "house" had crept into some 700 places across the code's
comments, the docs, the data notes and the page's copy before this session, and 200 more
during it. Swept to "OA" everywhere it meant an OA. Left alone: sponsor houses (a separate
concept in sponsors.js, unchanged), fighters' family houses in races.json, the OA named
"Alliance House", and the `custom_house` id (saved games carry it). Three identifiers the
sweep touched were restored by hand; the page helpers housePicker/shortHouse/houseNode are
oaPicker/shortOA/oaNode.

--- and appetite ---

RULED: the decision to give up is variable on the board's expectations, interest in the
planet's resources, the squads' health, the combats so far and strength now against
strength at the drop. `appetite` builds one number from those five and reads it into the
joiner's floor and gently the principal's ceiling; stance stays out (double-counting, per the
STANCE_LIFE_MULT note). Median 1.00, falling over the Divide as squads wear down; deals per
Divide unchanged. The strip says it as a word. The regret instrument shows appetite lets more
marginal folds close and the principal's ceiling loses those narrowly (10/19 right pinned,
8/22 live): the ceiling is too generous at the edge. Recorded, not tuned — it touches the
settlement's scale.

--- and one valuation for ransoms, captives and pacts ---

A ransom is asked at the body's worth to lose (pension + replacement, as bodyMoney prices it)
marked up and moved by regard; the owner pays up to 1.6× that, by appetite, from the treasury
it carries. A captor's living regard colours a captive's fate. A hungry OA wants no truce; an
OA that will not deal with you will not sign one. 129 ransoms in six Divides at a median ₡4.4k.

--- and the table's scenario gate ---

audit_table.cjs holds the table to nine rulings as a gate: whom a beaten OA approaches, whom a
principal courts, how rare cold alliances are, what memory does to price, which culture spite
and goodwill belong to, that a wounded OA wants out, that stance is not applied twice, that a
manager's banner is never bought for him, that the principal both refuses and takes. Two
behaviours fixed to pass it: contact now outranks a rich cold banner (RANK_CONTACT 1.0,
RANK_COLD 0.4), and principals no longer invite strangers.

--- and learning inside a career ---

At the settlement a joiner learns whether joining that banner paid what it signed for and a
principal learns whether buying that OA's help won; kept per pair across seasons
(persist.dealRecord) and read into the price at 10% a lesson, capped ±30%. Gated (T10).

THE MENU WEARS THREE OF THE FLEET'S FACES

- Three portraits — a Kellis, a Thythyn, an Etu — in TALL OVALS on the menu: a portrait's own
  shape, which keeps the antennae, ears and crest a circle would cut. The middle raised and a
  touch smaller; the sides lower and further out, framing the title from beneath and beside.
  Each wears the light of its own art.
- Foot line: "Eight Houses · One Year · One Divide", and beneath it THE EIGHT HOUSES' OWN
  MARKS in their own colours — the mock-up's placeholder glyphs, drawn for real by markFor.
- viewers/art/menu_{kellis,thythyn,etu}.webp (600x900, 183KB the three, from 3.4MB of PNG).
  build_corp.cjs reads them into ARX_DATA.art as data URLs so the one file opens anywhere.
- THE STAGE STACKS AND NEVER WRAPS. .overlay has flex-wrap:wrap, and in a column that means
  the moment the ovals plus the menu are taller than the screen the menu is pushed into a
  SECOND COLUMN to the right. .menustage is flex-wrap:nowrap and scrolls instead; the harness
  asserts the rule, since jsdom computes no layout.

--- and the Board ---

The Card stands across the top, each demand its own object with a gauge that moves and the
priority in the board's gold; the three that scale move below. A placement demand shows where

Fixes to that Board build: the holds' loss segments were absolutely placed in a bar that was

And the rest of the Board mockup, which had been agreed and not built: the boardroom strip with
patience on a banded bar and a word for it, the three that scale as needle dials, the audiences
with their band names, a month-ago tick and the change, the holds' tick and a foot that says

Who Is Watching to the mock: three columns with the reading in its own right-hand column, the
band names and month-ago tick on the bar, and one line of what moved each audience instead of a
dated block; the three in one box. The audcard styles are removed.
The audience bar is a fill on a plain track, coloured by its band, not a marker on a gradient:
.sbar is a slider, which is right for a picker and wrong for a standing.
The audience bar is its own class (.standfill): a block-level plain track filled to the reading
and coloured by its band. Wearing the picker's .sbar meant wearing its gradient (which won on
cascade order) and its inline display (which collapsed the bar to a stub and stacked the band
names). Band names print only where there is room; the reading drops "of 100".
And the colours: there is no global .bad rule in this stylesheet, so every band word came out
white; the Board names its own (bottom band red, the one above amber, good green, a fall red),
and the fill is one colour as the mock draws it — the bar says how much, the word how good.
Who Is Watching, read off the mock at last: the Board's section heads in the command colour,
the bar given the row's width, and two band names — the band below pulled left of its tick,
the band above pulled right — which cannot meet at any width.

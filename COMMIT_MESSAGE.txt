THE OVERHEAT IS RETIRED AND EVERY CELL HOLDS HALF AGAIN WHAT IT DID

A family that costs slightly more should perform slightly better; it was worse in nearly
every way instead — same money, softer at every tier a fleet fields, 37% fewer rounds a
fight, in exchange for freedom from a resupply burden that costs 3%.

- data/items.json: all fifteen cell-fed primaries lose `heat` and `heat_cap`, and every
  `charge` is multiplied by 1.5 (Surplus Las-Carbine 12 -> 18, Las-Repeater 18 -> 27).
- combat.js: spendShot no longer accumulates heat or sets `venting`. WHAT MAKES A WEAPON
  CELL-FED IS THAT IT HAS A CELL — isEnergy tested heatCap > 0, so pulling the overheat out
  of the catalogue alone would have stopped cells being spent at all and quietly turned every
  energy weapon into a ballistic one firing ammunition it does not carry. The family is named
  by `cellFed`. The venting machinery stays: a mod or quirk may still put heat in a weapon.
- VENT_EXCHANGES and HEAT_SUPPRESS_MULT labelled _RETIRED, not deleted — a heat rule may come
  back and this is where it lands. audit_code takes _RETIRED as a deliberate answer, as it
  takes [ABSTRACT]. VENT_EXCHANGES leaves arx.cjs's known-duplicates list.

MEASURED AFTER: a cell holds 19.5 shots, a fight asks 14.6, energy hands fire 14.6 rounds
against a ballistic hand's 13.5 — a 9% EDGE where there was a 37% deficit. Venting zero.
cellFedWithNoSidearm 22 -> 0. Range gradient unharmed: 2.25 dead a fight at a short opening
against 1.42 at a long one.

CAVEAT KEPT IN THE DOCS: the catalogue's charges were multiplied TWICE on the first attempt
(12 -> 18 -> 27) and caught only by checking the committed values. A data multiplier is not
idempotent and the file is its own only record.

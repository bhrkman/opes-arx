THE REPLAY: A PICTURE, NOT A WALL OF TEXT

A frame painted the page, not the canvas: every 16ms tick rewrote five panels and recomputed
vision. renderGround(false) paints the canvas alone, renderGroundPanels writes the page when
the day or state changes, and the walk runs on requestAnimationFrame.

A fight is the two OAs' marks, crossed — not a made-up three-letter tag and a result in words.
The words wait for the pointer, and a click opens the footage.

Zoom and pan: the wheel about the pointer, a drag, and three buttons; the terrain layer is
drawn once and scaled, so a zoom costs nothing.

A day announces itself over the map as a month does at the turn, so a press is not a silent
wait.

Gates: regress --fast 114/114 · drive · audit_ui · audit_resize · audit_docs · audit_code.

And the real cost, found by counting rather than reasoning: seenBySquads appends to G._seen on
every call and terrainLayer keys its cache on that array's length, so the frame loop rebuilt the
whole 900x900 terrain layer every frame. 40 rebuilds per 40 frames before, 0 after; a squad's
walk is built once and kept, 0 path builds per 40 frames.

A second pass from watching it: the walk began a whole interval late (setInterval's first tick),
which read as a jump back and a pause; a day's walk now fills the day's slot, eased both ends,
with the card ahead of it. You see who you are fighting — whoever your people met that day
stands on the map, solid and ringed — where the fog had hidden even a contact. The first window
opens at the drop and waits for a directive. One Drop button. Rounds read "undefined" because
DIVIDE.CONST.AMMO_LOAD does not exist; a squad's load is what its standing bodies carry.

A third pass, traced in the harness rather than reasoned: the corner's Drop hunted for a button
the Table had not drawn (so the first press did nothing); the window advance derived "watched so
far" from win.record.length, which the engine keeps appending to, so it jumped to the newest day
and skipped everything between; and the posted landings and the relay mast — two real reveals —
were unlabelled, so a sighting now carries `via` and the map says Posted Landing, Mast, or Seen.

The Desk — the eleven preparation months of a twelve-month year

You run one corporation. Every month is a turn: a budget of action points against verbs that compete, and the world only moves on once the month is ended. The other seven corps take their month at the same time, and the fixed dates — the Dividend, the Bastille intake, the merc deadline — fire at the close of the month that carries them, after everyone has spent.

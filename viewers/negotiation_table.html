Capital Divide · Step 6 · six baked Divides

Two forces end a Divide, and they work against each other. The ring closes until there is nowhere left to stand, and the banners consolidate as corps sell their claims to whoever looks likeliest to win. Whichever reaches one first is the ending. Every number below is lifted from a run of the sim.

The convergence

The band is the ring, drawn to scale as a share of the planet. The line is how many banners are still holding their own claim. A Divide ends when the line reaches one — by deals, by the grinder, or by both meeting at once.

What each side was looking at

Nobody negotiates against the truth. Both parties read the same public odds board, then price the same three things in credits: what staying is worth, what the crowd will charge for folding, and what fighting on will cost in people. A deal exists where the two ranges overlap.

Refused, and by how much

The closest misses. A refusal with numbers attached says what was wanted, what was on the table, and which side would have had to move.

Who ended up under whom

A corp cedes its claim once and cannot take it back. If its principal then joins someone else, it is dragged along with no say — and its share is paid out of a share.

Settlement

One winner takes the planet. Everyone else is paid strictly what they negotiated, out of what their principal actually received. Assay sites belong to whoever emptied them either way. Losers who joined nobody get nothing.

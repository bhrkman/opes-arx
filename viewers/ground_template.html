The Ground — the contest, drawn

This page runs a real Divide through the live modules — prng · roster · items · map · combat · tactical · negotiate · divide, inlined below and executed here — and draws the per-day recording that contest produced. It is not reading a captured file, so it cannot show you a Divide the engine would not play. The terrain is sampled from the planet's own terrainAt: it is the field the squads are actually walking through, not a texture. Nothing has drawn any of this before.

day 1
the wall — the solid circle is the closing line as it stands today. Nothing is outside it, because the field moves whatever it touches. The faint circle inside is where it closes to tomorrow.
squads — one dot each, sized by how many are still on their feet. The arrowhead is the way they are facing and the dotted line is where they are marching to, after the wall has clamped it back inside what their house is willing to risk. No arrow means they are holding where they stand. A gold ring means they are claiming that ground, and red rings mean a firefight held them there — one ring per two hours they could not march, claim, or be anywhere else while the wall kept closing.
sites — squares are revealed claims; filled means somebody holds it.

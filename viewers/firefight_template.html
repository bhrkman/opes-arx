The Firefight — one engagement, on the grid

The grid has recorded every body's tile, state, composure and ammunition once per acting fighter since it replaced the old band model, and nothing has ever drawn it. This page fights a real engagement with the live tactical.resolve and plays it back. It exists for THE DROP-IN: a squad that walks into a fight already in progress, entering on the bearing it approached from. That is the one thing here you cannot judge from a number — either you watch them come in behind people who set up facing somewhere else, or you are taking somebody's word for it.

bodies — filled circles are on their feet, hollow are down, crossed are dead. A ring means overwatch. The arriving squad flashes gold on the turn it walks in.

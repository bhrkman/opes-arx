The real grid resolver, one body at a time. Each fighter's turn is shown on the field as it stood when they chose — who they shot at, what the shot was worth, and what they had left afterwards. Nothing here is a recording.

Shaded squares are cover — darker is harder, and the hardest stops a shot entirely. A ring means overwatch; a red dot means suppressed. Lines are shots — green landed, grey missed. A gold wedge with a dashed circle is covering fire: nobody is hit, everyone inside it goes to ground. A dashed white line is ground this fighter just crossed — a long one is a dash: both actions spent moving, no shot and no cover, to reach a flank in one turn instead of three. Arrow keys step one body; Play runs the fight.

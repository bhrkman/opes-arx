The Year — eleven months that are not the Divide

Every figure below comes from the same modules the game runs, inlined and executed in this page: prng · roster · items · ledger · reputation · combat · tactical · negotiate · divide · season. Stepping a turn calls the real season loop. Nothing here is baked. Until Step 8.6 none of this existed — the whole year happened in one function that aged everybody, healed everybody to full, signed some people and stopped.

The calendar

Five two-month turns, then the lock, then the Divide. Each turn is five action points against verbs costing two, two, one and two — so a corp cannot treat its wounded, drill its green and survey the planet in the same two months. That competition is the management game.

What the year did to the people

The old offseason subtracted a flat 330 days from every wound in one pass, which cures everything this game can inflict — so no injury had ever survived into a second season and no manager had ever had to decide what to do about one. Mending now runs 61 days a turn, plus whatever treatment gets bought.

The events

T3 ends with the Dividend — a non-lethal show-match fought with real stun weapons, not cheap lethal ones. T4 is the Bastille intake, where the corp chooses. T5 is the merc deadline, where the fighter does.

Two different questions with two different sources: how many answers the board's funding demand, who answers the corp's own culture.

The Crate — what a sponsor's cache hands over

Every number and every item on this page is produced by the same modules the game runs: prng · roster · items · combat · tactical · divide, inlined below and executed here. The crate is opened by the real openCrate exported from divide.js, not by a copy of it — so this page cannot drift away from the engine the way a hand-written mirror would. The squad, as mustered

Eight bodies off the real generator, armed from the catalog the way a mid-table corp arms one. What each weapon is — its quirks, its damage type, the armour resistances behind it — is the whole of what Step 7.5 built.

What the old scalar did to it

Until Step 8.5 a looted cache set a squad-wide gearTier, and the resolver read that tier by replacing every fighter's weapon object with a bare {power, range, tier} and their armour with a bare {protection}. Struck through below is exactly what was discarded, for the rest of the Divide, from the squad that had just been rewarded. Measured across six Divides: 64 firings, 291 fighter-fights, 339 tags thrown away. This panel is an illustration of deleted behaviour — it is the one thing here that is not live, because the code no longer exists to run.

What it hands over now

One primary or armour piece at the best ordinary grade at or below the crate's tier, to whoever gains most by it, and only if it genuinely upgrades them — so an early tier-2 crate holds nothing a fielded squad wants and passes. Plus supplies. On a tier-5 crate, an exotic sometimes. Everything is a real catalog item, so it keeps its identity and is carried home into the armoury by whoever survives.

Is it worth walking to?

The squad that opened the crate against the same squad that did not, resolved on the grid, both directions with sides swapped, across five grounds. Positive means the crate helped. The project's own rule is that a number needs 200 fights a matchup across two agreeing populations — this runs live and small, so treat it as a demonstration of the machinery and read probe_crate.cjs for the real figure.
